During my **eight-week internship at AWS**, I gradually became familiar with the company’s **office environment, working processes, and professional working style**.

I had the opportunity to learn about and gain hands-on experience with services available on the **AWS Management Console**. I also participated in professional events to broaden my knowledge and gain practical experience.

In addition, I collaborated with other members to form a team, develop a workshop, and complete an application project on the AWS platform. The internship helped me strengthen my technical knowledge, improve my teamwork and time-management skills, and develop a professional working style.

During the eight-week internship, I completed the following main activities:

**Week 1:** [Became familiar with the office environment, working hours, and company processes; learned about the history and development of AWS.](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2:** [Studied learning materials and completed fundamental lessons on commonly used AWS services.](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3:** [Practiced using the AWS Management Console and learned how to use, manage, and monitor the costs of AWS services.](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4:** [Analyzed the project requirements, designed the system architecture, and selected suitable AWS services.](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5:** [Developed, integrated, and gradually completed the source code for the system’s main features.](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6:** [Deployed the project on AWS and developed workshop content covering the system’s design, development, and deployment process.](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7:** [Tested the complete system, resolved outstanding issues, and conducted a final project review.](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8:** [Completed the project, finalized the workshop content, and prepared the internship report.](1.8-week8/)
